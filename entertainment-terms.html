Entertainment Terms

Version 1.0 · 27 September 2026 · Applies to event organizers, creators and venue owners on SOKONI.

These terms describe how SOKONI's systems handle your events, content and money. They are operational terms, version 1.0, and are subject to review by SOKONI's legal team. When a new version is published you will be asked to accept it again; your earlier acceptance stays on record in the Legal Centre.

Event Organizer Agreement

  • You may sell tickets once SOKONI has approved your organizer application.
  • Every ticket for your event is sold through SOKONI — online, or at the door by staff you authorise. Selling tickets for a SOKONI event outside SOKONI is not permitted.
  • Each ticket has a unique ticket number and PIN. Admission is recorded against the PIN (or the optional QR code); a ticket admits once.
  • You set the event's capacity and ticket types. SOKONI will not sell more tickets than you made available.
  • You are responsible for delivering the event as described.

Ticketing & Refund Obligations

  • Before tickets go on sale you choose a refund policy: no refunds, or refunds until a deadline you set before the event, with or without refunds for tickets that were not used (no-shows).
  • Once a ticket has been sold, the policy cannot be changed for that event.
  • If you cancel the event, buyers are refunded.
  • Buyers request refunds through SOKONI with a reason. SOKONI checks the request against your policy and the ticket's admission record, and reviews it before any refund is paid. A ticket that was admitted is not a no-show.
  • Online refunds are paid through the original payment method. Tickets sold on your own card terminal are refunded by you, and the refund is recorded in SOKONI.
  • A penalty, if your policy has one, applies only to buyer-driven reasons and is shown to the buyer before they confirm a refund request.

Event Staff, Payments & Door Sales

  • You may give temporary staff access to one event, in a role you choose (cashier, admission, marketing or manager), for a time window that ends no later than 48 hours after the event. You can revoke it at any time.
  • Staff cannot withdraw money, change prices, approve refunds, change your payout details or access your other events.
  • SOKONI is the official ticketing and payment record for your event. Online purchase through SOKONI is the recommended way to sell.
  • Cash is not accepted for ticket sales. Every ticket is paid through SOKONI (M-PESA and other verified online methods) or, at the door, on your own card terminal.
  • Every door sale must be recorded in SOKONI by a named staff member. A card sale on your own terminal must include the terminal provider and transaction reference and match the ticket total; a reference can be used only once.
  • SOKONI charges 5% per ticket sale, deducted when the sale settles. Only paid, confirmed sales become withdrawable from your SOKONI wallet; an unpaid or unconfirmed ticket never does.
  • Talk to your buyers through SOKONI Messages — each order has its own conversation, which is part of the record.
  • You are responsible for the card payments your staff take on your terminal.

Commission

  • SOKONI charges 5% per ticket. For online sales it is calculated on the ticket value after the payment provider's fee; for door sales it is calculated on the ticket value.
  • The rate that applies to a sale is fixed when the sale is made.

Payment Settlement

  • Each ticket settles when your gate staff admit it with its PIN (or QR): that ticket's share of the online proceeds — ticket value, less the provider fee and SOKONI's 3% — is paid to your SOKONI business wallet at once, from which you can withdraw. It is never paid to the buyer.
  • Tickets that are never admitted settle 24 hours after your event ends. An unpaid or unconfirmed ticket is never paid out.
  • Proceeds are not paid while a refund on that order is open, or if the event was cancelled.
  • SOKONI's commission on door sales (which you collected directly) is deducted from your online proceeds before they are paid to your wallet.

Creator Content Agreement

  • Your titles are reviewed and approved by SOKONI before they are published. SOKONI may suspend a title that breaches these terms.
  • Buyers purchase or rent access to watch. Playback uses short-lived links and a moving watermark identifying the viewer. No system can make video impossible to copy; SOKONI does not claim otherwise.

Content Ownership Declaration

  • You declare that you own, or are licensed to distribute, everything you upload, including music and footage used in it, and that the people credited in your royalty agreement are entitled to their shares.

Royalty Settlement

  • From each sale the payment provider's fee is deducted first. SOKONI keeps 30% of the remainder; the remaining 70% is the creator pool, shared by the participants in the title's royalty agreement.
  • A royalty agreement is locked when the title is approved; changes apply only to later sales.
  • Royalties are calculated quarterly and paid to your SOKONI wallet. A refunded sale's royalty is reversed.

Venue Listing Agreement

  • Venue listings are reviewed by SOKONI before they are public, and may be suspended.
  • Booking requests sent through a venue listing are enquiries; SOKONI does not take payment for venue bookings today.

SOKONI Points Terms (v1.0)

  • Buyers earn 1 SOKONI point for every KES 10 they pay you in money; SOKONI calculates this from the completed sale.
  • 10 points are worth KES 1. A buyer may pay up to 25% of a sale with points.
  • When a buyer pays with points at your business, you fund that discount: you receive that much less money for the sale. SOKONI does not reimburse it, and the business where the points were earned does not pay it.
  • Points are spent only after the buyer confirms with a one-time code sent to their phone.
  • SOKONI’s commission is charged on the money you received, and tax figures treat points as your discount.
