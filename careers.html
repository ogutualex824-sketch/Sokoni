How to Apply

Send your CV and a short cover note explaining why you're excited about SOKONI and what you'd bring to the role:

Email: careers@mysokoni.co.ke

Subject: Application: [Role Name]

Include: CV/Resume, LinkedIn or portfolio (for design/engineering roles), 2–3 sentences on why SOKONI

We review all applications and respond to shortlisted candidates within 5–10 business days. We do not use application fees or require payment of any kind at any stage of our hiring process.

SOKONI will never ask job applicants to pay money or provide financial information as part of the application process. Any such request is a scam — report it to security@mysokoni.co.ke.
