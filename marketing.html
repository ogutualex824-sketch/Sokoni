💡 5 Tips to Sell More on Sokoni
01
Great Photos Sell
Listings with 3+ clear photos get 4× more clicks. Use natural light and show product from multiple angles.
02
Price Competitively
Check similar listings. Buyers compare prices. A small discount from market rate drives significantly more sales.
03
Respond Fast
Sellers who reply to messages within 1 hour close 3× more deals. Enable notifications so you never miss a buyer.
04
Use Flash Deals
A 24-hour flash sale creates urgency. Even a 10% discount during flash deals increases conversions by 60%.
05
Collect Reviews
Ask satisfied buyers to leave a review. 87% of shoppers read reviews before buying. Reviews build trust and rank your store higher.
