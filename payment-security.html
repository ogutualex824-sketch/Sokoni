How We Protect Your Payments

🔒

256-bit SSL Encryption

Every connection between your browser and SOKONI is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Your payment details are never transmitted in plain text.

🛡

IntaSend — PCI DSS Compliant

All card and M-Pesa transactions are processed by IntaSend, a CBK-regulated payment provider certified to PCI DSS standards.

🏦

Payment Escrow

Your money is held in escrow until you confirm delivery. Sellers receive payment only after you confirm the order — protecting you from fraud.

🔄

Idempotent Payments

Every payment request carries a unique key. Duplicate or double charges are impossible — the system rejects any repeat charge for the same order.

🚨

Real-Time Fraud Detection

Automated monitoring runs 24/7 — detecting unusual patterns, velocity anomalies, and suspected fraud before it reaches your account.

📋

Digital Receipts & Audit Trail

Every transaction generates a tamper-proof digital receipt with a unique reference and QR code. Verifiable at any time via payment-receipt.

📱

M-Pesa Native

M-Pesa payments go through Safaricom's secure STK Push — your PIN stays on your phone. SOKONI never sees your M-Pesa PIN.

👤

No Card Storage

SOKONI does not store your card number, CVV, or PIN on our servers. Card data is tokenised by IntaSend and processed in their PCI-DSS vault.

⚖️

CBK & KDPA Compliance

SOKONI operates within the framework of the Central Bank of Kenya's digital payments regulations and the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

Protecting Yourself from Scams

SOKONI will NEVER: ask for your M-Pesa PIN, bank password, or card number via email, phone, or chat. If you receive such a request claiming to be SOKONI, it is a scam.

Common Payment Scams — How to Avoid Them

🚩 Off-Platform Payments

If a seller asks you to pay via personal M-Pesa transfer or bank deposit outside SOKONI, refuse. Off-platform payments are not covered by Buyer Protection.

🚩 Fake Confirmation Messages

Scammers send fake M-Pesa confirmation screenshots. Always verify payment in your M-Pesa messages — not from what a seller shows you.

🚩 Too-Good-to-Be-True Prices

Listings priced far below market value for high-value items (phones, cars, laptops) are often scams. Use escrow payments and verify seller ratings.

🚩 Urgent Payment Requests

Pressure to pay quickly before an offer expires is a social engineering tactic. SOKONI never pressures buyers. Take your time; use the platform checkout.

Report suspected scams to security@mysokoni.co.ke or via the in-app "Report" button.
