💡 Tips to Maximize Your Earnings
01
Go Online at Peak Hours
Lunch (12–2PM) and dinner (6–9PM) are busiest. Evening deliveries often cover longer distances = bigger fees.
02
Stay in Hotspots
Westlands, CBD, and Eastlands generate the most orders. Position near restaurants and shopping areas.
03
Accept Quickly
Orders go to the nearest online driver. Fast accepts mean more deliveries per hour and better standing in the algorithm.
04
Maintain Your Rating
A 4.8+ rating unlocks priority orders. Be polite, deliver on time, communicate delays before the customer asks.
05
Pick the Right Orders
Lorry/pickup drivers: only accept freight-sized orders — the fee structure rewards you for long-distance bulk cargo. Boda riders: stick to parcels under 30km for the best time-to-earnings ratio.
06
Hit Weekly Milestones
Even 5 extra deliveries on Saturday/Sunday can push you into the next bonus tier and earn you an extra KES 500–2,000.
