7. Payments & Fees

Payment Methods

SOKONI accepts: M-Pesa (STK Push via IntaSend), debit/credit cards, and SOKONI Wallet. All transactions are processed in Kenya Shillings (KES).

Payment Processing

Payments are processed by IntaSend, licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya. SOKONI holds payments in escrow until order completion confirmation, then releases funds to the seller wallet minus commission.

Seller Payouts

  • Payouts are processed every Wednesday and Friday for confirmed completed orders.
  • Minimum payout threshold: KES 500.
  • Withholding Tax (WHT): 5% is deducted from seller payouts and remitted to KRA under Section 35 of the Income Tax Act, Cap. 470. A WHT certificate is issued to sellers monthly.
  • VAT-registered sellers: SOKONI deducts and remits 16% VAT on platform fees. Sellers are responsible for their own output VAT on goods sold.

Platform Fees

Platform fees (listing boosts, featured placements, subscriptions) are non-refundable unless SOKONI fails to deliver the service.
