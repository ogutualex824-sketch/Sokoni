🚀 Programs

Five Pillars.
One Vision: A Thriving Kenya.

Each program targets a specific barrier preventing young Kenyans from participating in the digital economy.

🎓
Sokoni Scholars
Digital Skills Academy
Full-scholarship digital skills programme for 100 top youth per cohort. Covers web design, e-commerce, digital marketing, data entry and online freelancing. 90-day intensive, fully online — no laptop required at start (we provide one).
🏆 100 scholars/cohort · 4 cohorts per year
💻 Laptop + airtime provided to every scholar
👩‍💼
She Sells
Women Entrepreneur Fund
Zero-interest grants of KES 5,000–50,000 for women-owned small businesses. Paired with a 6-month mentorship from successful Kenyan women entrepreneurs. Priority given to single mothers and first-time business owners.
💰 KES 5K – 50K per grantee · Zero interest
👯 6-month mentorship included for every recipient
🌐
Digital Villages
Rural Commerce Hubs
Physical digital access hubs in underserved areas — equipped with computers, Wi-Fi, printing, and a trained digital assistant. Goal: one hub in each of Kenya's 47 counties. Informal market vendors register on SOKONI for free at every hub.
🗺️ 18 hubs active · 29 more planned
📡 Free Wi-Fi + device access for all community members
🚀
Youth Inc.
Micro-Grant for Young Founders
Any Kenyan aged 18–30 with a business idea can apply. Grants of KES 5,000–25,000 with no repayment if business runs for 12 months. Business plan template, accounting tools, and seller onboarding to SOKONI all included.
🎯 Age 18–30 · No prior experience required
📋 Business plan support provided to every applicant
🤝
Trade Corps
Informal Market Digitisation
Partnering with county governments to train informal market vendors — Gikomba, Toi, Kongowea, Muthurwa — to sell online through SOKONI. Includes subsidised smartphones, 6-month data, and a dedicated seller buddy for the first 90 days.
🏪 680+ vendors already on SOKONI
📱 Subsidised phone + 6mo data for every new vendor
⭐
Foundation Ambassadors
Inspire. Mentor. Invest.
High-profile Kenyans — athletes, artists, business leaders, content creators — who pledge their visibility and voice to raise awareness, mentor scholars, and match-fund contributions. Each ambassador backs at least two beneficiaries personally.
🌟 14 ambassadors · Open to nominations
💬 Each mentors 2 beneficiaries directly per year

🗺️ Roadmap

Where We're Going

A five-year plan to make economic opportunity genuinely accessible to every Kenyan — regardless of county, gender, or education level.

Q1 2024 Foundation Launch Done
Foundation structure established, first 1% contribution mechanism live, 3 pilot Digital Village hubs opened in Nairobi, Mombasa and Kisumu.
Q3 2024 Sokoni Scholars — Cohort 1 Done
First 100 scholars graduated. 78% found employment or freelance income within 60 days of completion. 14 started their own digital businesses.
2025 She Sells Expansion + 18 Hubs In Progress
She Sells fund reaches KES 2M. Digital Village hubs reach 18 counties. Trade Corps onboards 1,000 informal vendors. Youth Inc first class graduates.
2026 Corporate Partnership Drive Upcoming
50 corporate partners. Foundation NGO registration complete (KE-NGO-2026). Annual Gala in Nairobi with impact showcase. First annual report published publicly.
2027 All 47 Counties — Full Coverage Vision
A Digital Village hub in every county. 5,000 scholars graduated. KES 20M in micro-grants disbursed. Foundation self-sustaining from SOKONI's 1% contribution alone.
