Daraja API Credentials
Get these from developer.safaricom.co.ke
📋 How to get your credentials (tap to expand)
  • 1
    Create a Safaricom Developer Account
    Visit the Safaricom Developer Portal and sign up with your business email.
    📋 developer.safaricom.co.ke
  • 2
    Create a New App
    In your dashboard → My Apps → Add a New App. Select "Lipa Na M-Pesa Online" as the product. Give it your business name.
  • 3
    Get Consumer Key & Secret
    On your app's detail page, click "Keys" tab. Copy the Consumer Key and Consumer Secret.
  • 4
    Get Your Passkey
    Go to M-Pesa Business → Lipa Na M-Pesa Online → Passkey. This is only available after going live. For sandbox, use the test passkey from the Daraja portal.
  • 5
    Register Callback URL
    In Daraja portal → Lipa Na M-Pesa Online → Validation URL. Paste SOKONI's callback URL below.
    📋 Copy Callback URL
🔒 Credentials are stored server-side only in Firebase Secret Manager. They are never sent to or stored in your browser beyond this form.
