How Commission Is Calculated

Example: You receive a payment of KES 5,000 on the Marketplace hub (3%):

Commission = 5,000 × 3% = KES 150

You keep = KES 4,850

Commission is invoiced monthly and paid via bank transfer or M-Pesa. You never have to send money in advance.

How Billing Works

1. You receive payments directly — customers pay into your own M-Pesa account. SOKONI never holds your money.

2. Commission is tracked automatically — every confirmed payment generates a commission record in your ledger.

3. Monthly invoice — on the 1st of each month, SOKONI issues an invoice for commissions from the previous month.

4. Payment terms — invoices are due by the 7th. Pay via M-Pesa Paybill 123456 (Account: your seller ID) or bank transfer.

5. Future — automatic commission collection directly from payments will be available once integrated with payment partners.
