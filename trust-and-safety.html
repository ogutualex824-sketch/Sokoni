What Buyer Protection covers

  • Item not received: If your order doesn't arrive within the stated delivery window, you can open a dispute and receive a full refund.
  • Item not as described: If the product you receive materially differs from the listing (wrong colour, model, size, or condition), you're entitled to a refund or replacement.
  • Counterfeit goods: Sale of fake or counterfeit items is prohibited. We cooperate with rights holders and remove infringing listings immediately.
  • Unauthorised charges: If you did not authorise a payment, contact us immediately. We investigate and refund confirmed unauthorised transactions.

How to claim Buyer Protection

  • Open your Order in the SOKONI app and tap "Problem with Order"
  • Select the issue type and provide evidence (photos, screenshots, messages)
  • Our team responds within 48 hours
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of approval

See our Refund Policy and Returns Policy for full terms.

Payment Security

Payments on SOKONI are handled exclusively by IntaSend, a Central Bank of Kenya-licensed payment processor. We never process card payments directly, and we never store card numbers or M-Pesa PINs.

  • All transactions are TLS 1.2+ encrypted in transit
  • M-Pesa STK Push: your PIN is entered directly on Safaricom's secure interface
  • Card payments are tokenised — only a token reference is stored by SOKONI
  • HMAC-SHA256 payment signatures prevent replay and tampering attacks
  • Idempotency keys prevent duplicate charges on network failures
  • Real-time velocity monitoring flags unusual spending patterns
  • Seller payouts require verified bank accounts or M-Pesa — no anonymous disbursements

Dispute Resolution

When a buyer and seller cannot resolve an issue directly, SOKONI provides structured mediation. Our goal is a fair outcome informed by evidence, not just the loudest complaint.

How disputes work

  • Either party can open a dispute within 30 days of order completion
  • Both parties are notified and given 48 hours to respond with evidence
  • A SOKONI mediator reviews messages, photos, delivery confirmations, and payment records
  • A decision is issued within 5 business days
  • Decisions can be appealed once within 7 days of the outcome

Possible outcomes

  • Full refund to buyer — item not received, item significantly not as described, or seller non-responsive
  • Partial refund — item partially as described; negotiated settlement
  • Decision in seller's favour — item delivered as described, buyer misrepresentation
  • Mutual cancellation — order cancelled by agreement before fulfilment

Seller consequences

Sellers with a dispute loss rate above 5% receive a performance warning. Above 10% triggers a mandatory review. Persistent violations result in suspension.

Fraud Prevention

SOKONI operates a layered anti-fraud system combining rule-based detection with AI monitoring. We protect both buyers and sellers from fraud.

What we detect

💳

Duplicate Charges

Automated detection flags the same amount charged to the same account within 5 minutes. Duplicates are rejected before the second charge completes.

⚡

Payment Velocity

Unusual payment volumes — more than 20 transactions per hour from a single merchant terminal — trigger a real-time alert and review.

🎭

Fake Reviews

Reviews are only accepted from verified buyers with completed transactions. Self-review, incentivised review, and review farming are detected and removed.

🔓

Account Takeover

Unusual login patterns, impossible travel, and credential stuffing attempts trigger automatic session revocation and re-authentication requirements.

🧾

Counterfeit Listings

AI scans new listings for known counterfeit signals — pricing far below market value, stock photos from known fake goods, brand name misuse — and flags them for human review.

Brute-force protection

Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. Unlocking requires email or SMS verification. IP-based rate limiting applies across all authentication endpoints.

Content Moderation

All listings, reviews, and user-generated content on SOKONI are subject to moderation. Our standards are set out in detail in the Community Guidelines.

Prohibited content

  • Counterfeit or intellectual property infringing goods
  • Dangerous, illegal, or regulated goods (weapons, narcotics, controlled substances)
  • Stolen goods or property
  • Adult content, explicit material, or content involving minors
  • Misleading pricing or deceptive product descriptions
  • Hate speech, harassment, or threatening content
  • Spam listings, duplicate accounts, or artificial inflation of ratings

Moderation process

  • AI pre-screening of all new listings before they go live
  • Human moderation queue for flagged content, reviewed within 24 hours
  • User reports reviewed within 48 hours
  • Immediate takedown for severe violations; seller notified and given right of appeal

Trust Levels & Verification Badges

SOKONI uses a Trust Passport system to communicate the verification level of every user on the platform. Badges are displayed on profiles, listings, and during checkout.

  • Verified Identity — National ID or passport confirmed
  • Verified Business — Business registration certificate confirmed
  • Verified Professional — Qualifications and professional credentials confirmed
  • KRA Compliant — KRA PIN and eTIMS registration confirmed
  • Highly Rated — 4.5+ star average rating with ≥20 completed orders

To start verification, visit your Profile and go to Settings → Verification, or visit the Verification Centre.

Reporting Abuse

Help us keep SOKONI safe by reporting anything suspicious. Your report is confidential and reviewed by a human within 24 hours.

How to report

  • On a listing: tap the three-dot menu and select "Report Listing"
  • On a seller profile: tap "Report Seller" at the bottom of their profile page
  • On a review: tap the flag icon next to the review
  • On a message: long-press the message and tap "Report"
  • By email: trust@mysokoni.co.ke with evidence

Emergency safety situations

If you believe you are in immediate danger as a result of a transaction on SOKONI, contact the Kenya Police Service on 999 or 0800 722 203 immediately. Then contact us at security@mysokoni.co.ke so we can cooperate with law enforcement.

Intellectual property infringement

Rights holders may submit takedown requests to legal@mysokoni.co.ke. Include the infringing listing URL, proof of ownership, and a statement of good faith. We respond within 3 business days.
