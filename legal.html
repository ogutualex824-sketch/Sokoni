🍪 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. SOKONI uses cookies and similar technologies (such as localStorage and sessionStorage) to make the Platform work and to understand how you use it.

🍪 Types of Cookies We Use

🔧 Essential Cookies (Always On)

Required for the Platform to function. These cannot be disabled.

  • sokoniUser — stores your login session
  • cart — stores your shopping cart
  • wishlist — stores saved items
  • sokoniAgeVerified — stores age verification result (session only)
  • sokoniDemoSeeded — records that demo data has been loaded

📊 Analytics Cookies (Opt-In)

Used to understand Platform usage via Google Analytics 4.

  • _ga, _gid, _gat — Google Analytics session tracking (anonymised IP)
  • sokoniAnalytics — local analytics data stored on your device only

Google Analytics data is anonymised and processed under Google's data processing terms.

🔔 Notification Cookies

  • sokoni_fcm_token — stores your push notification token
  • sokoniNotifDismissed — records if you dismissed the notification prompt

⚙️ Managing Cookies

You can clear all SOKONI cookies at any time:

  • Browser settings: Clear site data for mysokoni.co.ke
  • Mobile (Android/iOS): Browser Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data
  • PWA installed: App Settings → Clear data

Clearing essential cookies will log you out and clear your cart.

Note on localStorage: SOKONI stores most data in browser localStorage rather than traditional cookies. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you are logged in and syncing to Firebase.
