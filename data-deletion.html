⚡ Delete My Account Instantly

If you are signed in to SOKONI, you can delete your account and all associated personal data directly from your profile settings — no waiting required.

Go to Profile Settings

Tap your profile picture → Settings → Account

Select "Delete Account"

Scroll to the danger zone at the bottom of Account settings

Confirm Deletion

Type DELETE to confirm — action is irreversible

Done

Your account is deactivated immediately. Permanent purge completes within 30 days

Go to Profile Settings

🗑️ What Gets Deleted

When you delete your SOKONI account, the following is permanently removed within 30 days:

  • Profile name, photo, email address, phone number
  • Order history and delivery addresses
  • Reviews written and received
  • Messages and chat history
  • Saved items, wishlists, search history
  • Wallet balance (disbursed before deletion, if any)
  • Seller listings and storefront
  • All device tokens and notification preferences
⚠️ Legal retention: Financial transaction records and KRA eTIMS receipts are retained for 7 years as required by Kenyan tax law, even after account deletion. These records are anonymised where possible.
