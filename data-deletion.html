Delete Your Account via the App

The fastest way to delete your SOKONI account and data is directly from within the app. No email or form required.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes your profile, listings, saved items, and personal data from SOKONI. Financial and tax records required by Kenyan law are retained for 7 years.

  1. Open SOKONI on your phone or browser and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Profile icon (bottom navigation bar, person icon).
  3. Tap Settings (gear icon at the top right of your profile page).
  4. Scroll to the Privacy section and tap "Delete Account".
  5. Read the confirmation warning carefully. Type DELETE in the confirmation box when prompted.
  6. Your account will be immediately deactivated. Personal data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

If you cannot access your account (e.g. you have deleted the Facebook app and cannot sign in), use the Data Rights Request Form below or email privacy@mysokoni.co.ke with your registered email address or Facebook User ID.

What Gets Deleted vs. Retained

Permanently Deleted (within 30 days)

  • Your name, email, phone number
  • Profile photo and bio
  • Login credentials
  • Social login connections (Facebook, Google)
  • Saved addresses and payment methods
  • Chat and messaging history
  • Wishlist and saved items
  • Search history and activity logs
  • Push notification tokens
  • Marketing preferences
  • Device identifiers
  • Account preferences and settings

Retained by Law

  • Order records (7 years — Kenya Income Tax Act)
  • KRA eTIMS invoices (7 years — KRA obligation)
  • Payment & transaction records (7 years — CBK)
  • WHT certificates issued to you
  • Dispute records involving third parties
  • Security audit logs (2 years)

Retained records are anonymised where possible and inaccessible to platform staff without a lawful basis.

Deletion Timeline

Immediately
Your account is disabled. You cannot sign in. Your profile is removed from public view. Active sessions are revoked.
Within 24 Hours
Your listings are removed from the marketplace. Your profile photo and public data are purged from the CDN.
Within 14 Days
Personal data is deleted from primary databases. Chat history is removed. Notification tokens purged.
Within 30 Days
All personal data is permanently deleted from backup systems. The deletion is complete. A confirmation email is sent (if your email address is still accessible).
7 Years
Legally required financial and tax records are retained per the Kenya Income Tax Act Cap. 470 and KRA eTIMS regulations, then permanently deleted.

Facebook Login Data Deletion

If you connected SOKONI using Facebook Login, Facebook may notify us when you remove SOKONI from your Facebook apps or request data deletion via Facebook's settings. We process these automated requests within 30 days via our secure data deletion callback.

How to remove SOKONI from Facebook

  1. Visit facebook.com/settings/applications (or tap Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites on the Facebook app).
  2. Find SOKONI in the list of connected apps.
  3. Tap Remove and then confirm.
  4. Facebook will automatically send a deletion request to SOKONI. We will process it within 30 days.

Alternatively, you can delete your SOKONI account directly via the self-service method above, which also disconnects your Facebook login connection.

Our automated Facebook data deletion callback endpoint is available at:

POST https://mysokoni.co.ke/api/facebook/data-deletion

This endpoint is registered with Meta and receives deletion notifications directly from Facebook's servers. It verifies the request signature using HMAC-SHA256 before processing.
