💡 Tips for Your First Sale

📸
Use real photos. Products with 3+ clear, well-lit photos sell 4× faster than those without. Use natural light, show all angles.
💰
Price competitively. Search for similar products on SOKONI and price within 10% of competitors. You can always adjust later.
📝
Write detailed descriptions. Include size, colour, condition, and what makes your product special. Answer buyer questions before they ask.
⚡
Reply fast. Sellers who reply to messages within 1 hour convert 60% more inquiries into sales. Enable notifications.
✅
Get verified. The ✅ verified badge builds instant trust. Verified sellers earn 3× more on average. Apply now — it's free.
