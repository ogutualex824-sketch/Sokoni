4. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide the service: Process orders, facilitate payments, match buyers and sellers.
  • Authentication: Verify your identity, maintain session security, enforce account limits.
  • Logistics: Route deliveries, share your address with the assigned driver, provide live tracking.
  • Payments: Process M-Pesa, card, and wallet transactions; generate eTIMS-compliant receipts.
  • Communications: Send order notifications, delivery updates, marketing emails (with consent).
  • Trust & Safety: Detect fraud, prevent abuse, enforce community standards.
  • Legal compliance: Meet KRA eTIMS obligations, anti-money-laundering requirements, court orders.
  • Platform improvement: Aggregate analytics to improve search, recommendations, and performance.

6. Payments & Financial Data

Payment processing is handled by IntaSend (licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya). SOKONI does not store raw card numbers or M-Pesa PINs.

  • M-Pesa STK push is initiated via IntaSend — your PIN is entered directly on the Safaricom interface.
  • Transaction records (amount, reference, status) are stored in our Firestore ledger for accounting and dispute resolution.
  • Seller payouts are processed via IntaSend B2C with Withholding Tax (WHT) applied at 5% per Kenya Revenue Authority regulations.
  • KRA eTIMS receipts are generated for all taxable transactions.

IntaSend Privacy Policy: intasend.com/privacy

7. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how the platform is used. All analytics data is aggregated and anonymised before being used to make product decisions. We do not use analytics data to build individual advertising profiles.

We also maintain internal platform analytics (search trends, funnel metrics, seller performance scores) stored in Firestore, accessible only to authorised admin users.

8. Data Sharing

We share personal data only as required:

  • Sellers: Your delivery name and address are shared with the seller who fulfilled your order.
  • Drivers: Your pickup/delivery coordinates are shared with the assigned driver during active delivery.
  • Firebase / Google: Authentication and database infrastructure.
  • IntaSend: Payment processing (name, phone number, amount).
  • KRA: eTIMS transaction data as required by law.
  • Law enforcement: When legally required by a valid Kenyan court order or statutory demand.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes.

10. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Restriction: Request that we limit processing of your data.
  • Withdraw consent: At any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, visit our Data Deletion & Rights Centre or email privacy@mysokoni.co.ke.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at odpc.go.ke.

11. Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2+.
  • Firestore data is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud.
  • Payment credentials are stored in Firebase Secret Manager (not in code).
  • Admin access is protected by multi-factor authentication and role-based access control.
  • We conduct regular security audits and penetration testing.
  • Brute-force protection: accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts.

If you discover a security vulnerability, please disclose it responsibly to security@mysokoni.co.ke.

12. Children

SOKONI is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at privacy@mysokoni.co.ke and we will delete it.
