Delete Account

Permanently delete your SOKONI account and all associated data.

Step 1 of 5
Why are you leaving?
Your feedback helps us improve SOKONI for everyone.
Step 2 of 5
What you'll lose
This action cannot be undone after 30 days.

Deleting your account will permanently remove:
• Your profile, orders, and purchase history
• All messages and conversations
• Wallet balance and loyalty points
• Business listings and seller ratings
• Any pending payouts or refunds
You have a 30-day grace period after requesting deletion. Sign in again during this period to cancel.
Step 3 of 5
Resolve pending items
Before deleting, please resolve any of the following items. These cannot be processed after account deletion.
No pending orders
No active disputes
No outstanding payouts
Step 4 of 5
Download your data first
We recommend downloading a copy of your data before deleting your account.
Step 5 of 5
Confirm account deletion
Your account will be scheduled for deletion.
You have 30 days to sign in and cancel. After 30 days, all your data will be permanently erased.
