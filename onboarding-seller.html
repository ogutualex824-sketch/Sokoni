Commission — two separate rates

Marketplace orders (orders SOKONI brings you) depend on your plan: Free 15% · Basic 10% · Pro 5% · Enterprise 0%.

In-shop POS / Till sales — sales you make yourself at your own counter — are a flat 5% on every plan. Your plan changes your marketplace rate; it does not change your till rate.

A minimum commission of KES 10 per sale applies, except where your plan’s rate is 0%.

Full terms, including how and when commission is collected, are in the Seller Agreement.
