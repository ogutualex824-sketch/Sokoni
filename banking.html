📲 Get your business on M-Pesa today

Every Sokoni seller should have a Paybill or Till Number. Accept payments 24/7, track transactions, reconcile easily & build transaction history for loan applications.

📲 How to Set Up M-Pesa Business
1
Register Business
Have your business registration certificate, KRA PIN & national ID ready. Individual businesses can also register with personal ID.
2
Visit Safaricom or Apply Online
Go to any Safaricom shop or visit M-Pesa Business at safaricom.co.ke/m-pesa-business. Sokoni can facilitate the application for you.
3
Choose: Paybill or Till
Paybill: customer enters account number (good for multiple products). Till: fixed amount button (good for physical shops). Both are free.
4
Link to Bank Account
Link your Paybill to your business bank account for daily auto-settlements. Takes 2–5 business days to activate.
5
Go Live on Sokoni
Add your Paybill/Till to your Sokoni seller profile. All your customers can now pay via M-Pesa checkout seamlessly.
6
Build Loan History
6 months of M-Pesa transactions qualifies you for KCB M-Pesa, Equity Eazzy & Fuliza business loans with no physical collateral.

👥 Chamas — Kenya's Wealth-Building Secret

Over 300,000 registered chamas in Kenya manage KES 300 billion+ in assets. A chama is a self-help investment group — members pool money monthly, invest collectively & take turns accessing capital. SOKONI makes running your chama digital & transparent.

🔄 How a Chama Works
1
Form Your Group
3–30 members. Set monthly contribution amount (e.g. KES 1,000–50,000/member). Elect chairperson, treasurer & secretary.
2
Contribute Monthly
Every member contributes monthly via M-Pesa. SOKONI tracks contributions, sends reminders & flags missed payments automatically.
3
Pool & Invest
Pool funds into: Money Market Funds (10–15% p.a.), Treasury Bills, land purchase, shares on NSE, or a group business/rental property.
4
Access Chama Loans
Members can borrow up to 3× their savings from the chama fund at 0–5% interest. Repaid back into the pool — no bank required.
5
Bank Group Loans
With 6+ months of records, your chama qualifies for group loans from KCB, Equity & Co-op Bank (up to KES 10M with group guarantee).
6
Grow Wealth Together
Annual AGM: distribute dividends, review investments, elect new officials. Many chamas own apartments, schools & businesses within 5 years.
