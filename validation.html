🟡 “Queued” is not “delivered.”
A push that the server accepted, an email that entered the queue, an SMS the provider returned 200 for — none of those are evidence that anything arrived. They stay 🟡 here until you confirm on the device. That distinction is exactly what hid a months-long push outage behind a green dashboard.
