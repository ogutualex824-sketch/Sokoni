# Category Launch Targets

**Phase:** Phase 0 — Market Activation  
**Purpose:** Minimum inventory thresholds before each category is promoted  
**Review Cadence:** Weekly (every Monday)

---

## Rule

Do not advertise, feature, or promote any category that has not met its minimum listing count.

An empty-looking category destroys buyer confidence. It is better to not show a category than to show it empty.

---

## Launch Targets

### Priority Tier 1 — Must Hit Before Any Marketing

| Category | Min Listings | Target | Seller Count | Notes |
|----------|-------------|--------|--------------|-------|
| Electronics | 100 | 200 | 6–10 sellers | TVs, laptops, audio, accessories |
| Fashion | 150 | 300 | 10–15 sellers | Men, women, children — local brands prioritised |
| Phones & Accessories | 100 | 200 | 5–8 sellers | New + second-hand; covers covers, cables, chargers |
| Beauty & Personal Care | 75 | 150 | 5–8 sellers | Skincare, hair, make-up — include Kenyan brands |
| Home & Kitchen | 100 | 200 | 6–10 sellers | Cookware, cleaning, décor, appliances |
| Groceries | 100 | 200 | 4–6 sellers | Dry goods, cereals, snacks — no perishables at launch |

### Priority Tier 2 — Must Hit Before Category-Specific Marketing

| Category | Min Listings | Target | Seller Count | Notes |
|----------|-------------|--------|--------------|-------|
| Automotive | 75 | 150 | 5–8 sellers | Spare parts, accessories, oils, tyres |
| Services | 50 | 100 | 10–20 providers | Plumbers, cleaners, tutors, designers |
| Healthcare | 20 | 40 | 5–10 providers | Clinics, pharmacies, labs, wellness |
| Events | 10 | 25 | 5+ organisers | Concerts, sports, workshops, conferences |
| Cars & Vehicles | 30 | 60 | 5–10 sellers | Both new and second-hand; include boda bodas |

### Priority Tier 3 — Can Launch Later

| Category | Min Listings | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Jobs | 25 | Only after 50+ active sellers have hiring needs |
| Property | 20 | Coordinate with estate agents |
| Education | 15 | Online courses, tutors, training centres |
| Legal Services | 10 | Advocates, paralegals, notaries |

## What to Do When a Category is Below Target

1. Identify sellers who could fill this category (use the outreach sheet)
2. Contact them directly with a category-specific pitch
3. Offer a bonus incentive for the first seller who hits 30+ products in that category
4. Consider listing products on behalf of physical market traders (with their permission)
5. Do not turn on advertising for that category until it hits 🟢

## Product Quality Requirements by Category

### Electronics
- Model number in title
- Condition clearly stated (New / Refurbished / Used)
- At least 2 product photos (front + back)
- Warranty information in description

### Fashion
- Size chart or measurements in description
- At least 2 photos (front + back, or on model)
- Material stated
- Local Kenyan sizing used where applicable

### Groceries
- Weight/volume clearly stated
- Expiry management — seller must commit to weekly stock updates
- No loose items without packaging photos

### Healthcare
- Provider qualifications in description
- Physical address or telehealth status stated
- No medical advice or diagnostic claims in descriptions
