## The two fields are not synonyms

| Field | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **owner** | Accountable for the integration operating correctly, and for
operational follow-up when it fails. |
| **authority** | Authorised to approve material changes — credentials,
lifecycle, configuration. |

The person who responds to a failed payment rail may not be the person who may
rotate its production credential or reopen a frozen one.

## Legal values for a decision column

| Value | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `<UNASSIGNED>` | **Undecided.** The default, and a gate failure once the
schema lands. |
| a named party | Decided. |
| `NONE — RAIL CLOSED` | **Decided**, and valid for `owner` ONLY on a closed
rail: nobody is operationally accountable because nothing is operating. |

> `NONE — RAIL CLOSED` is never valid for **authority**. A closed rail still
> needs someone who may decide to reopen it — closed is a lifecycle state, not
> an ownership exemption.
