# Soft Launch Criteria

**Phase:** Phase 0 — Market Activation  
**Purpose:** Objective go/no-go checklist before significant advertising spend  
**Decision authority:** Founder / Operations Lead  
**Review:** Weekly — check progress against each metric

---

## Principle

Advertising before the marketplace is ready accelerates failure.

A buyer who comes to an empty marketplace, finds no results, or has a bad first experience will not return — even after the marketplace improves. First impressions are permanent.

Do not start paid marketing until every criterion below is met.

## What "Active" Means

- **Active seller:** A store that is verified, has ≥20 products, is visible in search, and has logged in within the last 14 days
- **Active listing:** A product with price > 0, at least 1 photo, in stock, not manually hidden
- **Active event:** An event with a future date, ticket price or RSVP link, and a description

---

## Staged Marketing Approach

Do not go from zero to full advertising. Move in stages:

### Stage 1 — Internal Soft Launch
**Trigger:** 10+ active sellers, 200+ listings  
**Actions:**
- Share the marketplace with friends, family, and business contacts only
- Use personal networks (WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn)
- No paid ads
- Gather feedback; fix anything that feels wrong

### Stage 2 — Community Launch
**Trigger:** 25+ active sellers, 500+ listings, ≥6 categories ready  
**Actions:**
- Post in relevant WhatsApp groups (business, buyers, community)
- Share on Instagram and Facebook with real product photos
- Invite early buyers to leave reviews
- Still no paid ads

### Stage 3 — Soft Public Launch
**Trigger:** All criteria in this document are met  
**Actions:**
- Small paid campaign (Facebook/Instagram) — KES 500–2,000/day test budget
- Google Business Profile optimisation
- Press release to Kenyan tech blogs (TechCabal, Techweez, Disrupt Africa)
- SMS campaign to existing contact list

### Stage 4 — Growth Marketing
**Trigger:** First 100 orders completed, buyer satisfaction ≥ 4.0/5.0  
**Actions:**
- Increase ad spend based on cost per acquisition data
- Influencer partnerships
- Loyalty programme activation (if evidence gate met — see V2_ROADMAP)
- Category-specific campaigns

---

## Disqualifying Events

Pause any marketing (even word of mouth) if:
- Payment processing goes down
- More than 5 open P1 bugs at once
- A data breach or security incident
- Average listing quality drops below 65
- A major seller suddenly removes all their products (causes empty categories)

---

## Post-Launch Monitoring

After Stage 3 begins, review daily:
- Homepage freshness (are new products appearing?)
- Search quality (top queries returning relevant results?)
- Order completion rate (orders placed → orders fulfilled)
- Buyer feedback (any sudden spike in complaints?)
