## If You Hit Blockers

**No one replies to your messages:**
- Your message may be too formal. Shorten it — 3 sentences max.
- Call instead of messaging. A voice call converts better.
- Try a different channel — Instagram DM if WhatsApp isn't getting responses.

**Seller agrees but keeps postponing:**
- Send a specific time: "Are you free Thursday at 3pm? I'll call you."
- Do not chase more than twice. Move to next prospect.

**Seller struggles with product photos:**
- Suggest natural light from a window, product on a plain white cloth or paper
- For clothing: hang it or lay it flat, photograph from above
- First listing does not need to be perfect — good enough now beats perfect never

**Platform shows an error during onboarding:**
- Screenshot the error, message me immediately with the browser console output
- I will fix it same day — this is exactly the kind of bug that blocks the launch
