💡 Tips for New Drivers

🛡️
Safety first. Always verify the rider's name and destination before starting a trip. Keep your doors locked until the trip begins. Use the SOS button if needed.
⚡
Accept quickly. Drivers who accept within 10 seconds get 30% more trip requests. A fast accept rate boosts your ranking on the platform.
⭐
Rating matters. Maintain 4.5+ stars to stay on the platform. Be friendly, keep the car clean, and offer phone chargers — small things earn 5-star reviews.
📄
Keep documents current. Expired insurance or driving licence = immediate suspension. Set calendar reminders 30 days before each document expires.
💰
Peak hours pay more. Morning (6–9am) and evening (5–8pm) have the highest surge. Work these windows consistently to double your daily earnings.

💸 How Earnings Work

You keep 85% of every fare. SOKONI's 15% covers dispatch, insurance, and platform costs.

Payouts are sent to your M-Pesa every Friday (or daily if you selected daily payout during signup). No hidden deductions — what you see is what you get.
