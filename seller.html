📋 Business Permits & Licenses Optional

Adding your business license and permits shows buyers you are a verified, legitimate shop. You get a ✅ Verified Merchant badge visible on all your listings. Completely optional — you can skip this step and add later.

🏛️

Nairobi City County & Government of Kenya

Official business permits are issued by the Nairobi City County (formerly City Council). All permits can be applied for online at eCitizen — links below take you directly to the correct portal.

🇰🇪
KRA PIN Certificate
Kenya Revenue Authority
✅ Uploaded
Your KRA Personal Identification Number is required for business registration. Every business selling above KES 5M annually must be VAT registered. Your PIN also links your business to the tax system.
↗️ Apply on iTax →
📜
Single Business Permit (SBP)
Nairobi City County — Annual License
✅ Uploaded
The Single Business Permit is the primary business operating license issued by Nairobi City County. Required for all businesses operating within Nairobi. Costs from KES 5,000/year for small businesses. Renewed annually by January 31st.
↗️ Apply on eCitizen →
🏢
Business Registration / Certificate of Incorporation
Business Registration Service (BRS)
✅ Uploaded
Register your business name or company with the Business Registration Service. A sole proprietorship (business name) costs KES 950 online via Huduma/eCitizen. A limited company costs KES 17,000+. Required to open a business bank account.
↗️ Register at BRS →
🔥
Fire Safety Certificate
Nairobi City County — Fire Department
✅ Uploaded
Required for businesses with physical premises in Nairobi. Inspectors check fire exits, extinguishers, escape routes and electrical safety. Applies to restaurants, warehouses, salons, shops over 20sqm. Annual renewal required.
↗️ Apply on eCitizen →
🏥
Public Health Certificate Food Businesses
Nairobi City County — Public Health
✅ Uploaded
Mandatory for all food-handling businesses including restaurants, food delivery kitchens, grocery stores, butcheries and cafeterias. Staff food-handler certificates also required. Annual public health inspection conducted.
↗️ Apply on eCitizen →
