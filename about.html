Our Mission

SOKONI is a digital commerce ecosystem developed, owned and operated by Bravilex International Co. Limited. Our mission is to build Africa's most trusted digital marketplace by connecting buyers, sellers, service providers, logistics partners, and businesses through secure technology.

SOKONI exists to make commerce, services, and opportunity accessible to every Kenyan — whether you're a buyer in Nairobi, a seller in Kisumu, a service provider in Mombasa, or a driver anywhere in between.

We build technology that removes the friction between people who need things and people who provide them — creating a fair, fast, and trusted digital ecosystem for Kenya's economy.

"SOKONI" means market in Swahili. It is who we are.

Our Journey

1
2025 — Concept
SOKONI was conceived to address the fragmentation of Kenya's digital commerce — too many apps, too little trust, too much friction.
2
Early 2026 — Foundation
Core marketplace, authentication, payments (M-Pesa via IntaSend), and logistics infrastructure built and tested.
3
Mid 2026 — Platform Expansion
Launched Food Hub, Property, Events, SmartPOS, FinOS financial system, and AI-powered search. Enterprise security certified at 95/100.
4
June 2026 — Public Launch
SOKONI opens to the public. 600+ Cloud Functions live. Real-time delivery, KASS AI Concierge, and full payment trust layer deployed.
→
2027 & Beyond
Expanding to East Africa. Deeper eTIMS compliance, AI personalisation, B2B trade network, and regional logistics partnerships.
